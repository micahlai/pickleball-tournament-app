# Provisional brand guidelines

Status: temporary creative direction authorized by the user; name and logo are intended to be replaced.

## Name and logo

Working name: **Courtwave**. It references tournament courts and waves. This is a temporary project label, not a claim of name/domain availability.

Asset: [transparent logo](../assets/brand/courtwave-logo.png). A C-shaped mark combines a pickleball and wave strokes beside a wordmark. Generated using the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-16. It is a raster concept, not a production vector or app-store icon.

Keep its aspect ratio, use on a light uncluttered surface, and allow space around the mark. Do not derive product architecture, domain names, or permanent app identifiers from the temporary name. Replace this asset and the name together when the user supplies the final identity.

## Voice

Proposed voice: clear, short, calm, operational. Use “Wave,” “Division,” “Court,” “Match,” “Score,” and “Standings” consistently. Say what happened and what to do next. Keep player-facing screens focused on where to play and current results.

Example labels: “View schedule,” “Enter score,” “Move match,” “Resolve tie.” Example network feedback: “Score not saved. Reconnect and try again.”

## Logo generation prompt

Built-in image-generation mode; full prompt:

> Create one temporary logo asset for a pickleball tournament planning app named Courtwave. Transparent background, centered compact horizontal lockup: distinctive simple geometric C-shaped court/wave emblem with a small perforated pickleball, and the exact word Courtwave in a clean highly legible modern sans-serif wordmark to its right. Flat vector-like raster artwork, restrained and functional, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no slogan. Palette: primary slate #5F6582, optional muted teal #5F7B82, warm sand accent #FFD791, periwinkle accent #91A4FF; earthy #81745E only if needed. Wordmark in #5F6582 for a light background. Use few solid shapes and strong negative space, readable at small size. Wide composition with comfortable transparent margins. This is a provisional replaceable design, not a finished brand identity.
