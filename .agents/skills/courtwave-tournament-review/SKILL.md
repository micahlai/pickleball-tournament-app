# Courtwave tournament review

Read [tournament rules](../../../specs/tournament-rules.md), [scheduling](../../../specs/scheduling.md), [source notes](../../../docs/source-notes.md), and [open decisions](../../../docs/open-decisions.md).

Use stable team identity separately from random schedule position and standings rank. Random slot numbers never affect score or tie resolution.

Check seven-team reference ordering, 21 distinct pairings, six games per team, and one bye each. For the eight-team initial reference, check 12 within-pool pairings and three games per team over four rounds. The manager chooses initial pools only or a second reseeded stage with all six games per team in one division score record. Do not invent an all-eight-team round robin or a default format. Final placement is manager-selectable: separate winners/consolation ranges or all eight ranked together. Extra tiebreak games never enter main division totals.

For 4–6-team divisions, format is manager-selected. Ordinary round robin is a proposed option; do not invent the rest of the format menu. Check court and team overlaps, optional player overlaps if participation is clarified, byes, and constraints during manager rearrangements.

Do not equate “three games per team” with “three rounds.” More courts do not automatically permit a different pairing order.

Managers choose points targets and ranking rules; the ranking default is most wins → fewest total points conceded → head-to-head. A remaining three-way tie permits a manager-selected three-game mini round robin or shared rank. Extra tiebreak games only resolve tied places and leave main wins/losses and point totals unchanged. Alternative ranking options, multi-team head-to-head behavior, residual ties, and shared-rank numbering remain open.

Report each finding with its rule/source, concrete schedule or score example, consequence, and decision needed. Use synthetic identities. Do not use the PDF's illustrative historical ranks as test truth.

Current authorization covers M0/M1 foundations. Domain feature implementation remains gated to M2–M4. Future verification should target domain invariants and approved examples rather than testing that code repeats its own formula.
